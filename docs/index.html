Get started

Up and running in under a minute.

  1. Open the Add-ins store

    In Excel, go to Home › Add-ins. On older versions it's Insert › Get Add-ins.

  2. Add MEx Automate

    Search for MEx Automate and click Add. It's free, and there's nothing to sign up for.

  3. Click Automate

    A new Automate button appears on the Home tab. Click it, pick a tool, and follow the steps.

Questions

Before you install.

Which versions of Excel does it work with?

Excel 2019 or later on Windows and Mac, Excel in Microsoft 365, and Excel on the web.

Will it change my original data?

Only where you ask it to. Clean up data changes the cells shown in its "Working on" bar. Combine sheets and charts put their results on new sheets and leave the originals alone. Update a report replaces only the data rows you marked, and keeps titles, totals and formula columns.

Can I undo a change?

Excel's Undo may not reverse every change an add-in makes, so for a workbook that matters, save a copy before a big clean-up. Combining and charting never touch your original sheets.

Does it need macros or VBA?

No. It's built on Microsoft's modern add-in platform, so there are no macros to enable and no security warnings to click through.

Does it need the internet?

Excel loads the add-in itself from the web when you open it, the same as any Office add-in. Your spreadsheet data is never sent anywhere.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no subscription and no account.
